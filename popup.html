HOW TO READ THE CITY
Each building = one day
Every block on the grid represents a single calendar day. If you made at least one submission that day, it becomes a glowing tower.
Floors = submissions that day
Every floor of a tower is one submission. Taller towers mean more problems solved that day (capped at 20 floors).
Same color = same streak
Consecutive coded days share a neon color, so a continuous streak reads as one glowing district. A new color starts when you miss a day and begin a new streak.
Platform height = streak length
Towers in a long streak sit on a taller platform. The longer the streak, the higher the platform — a subtle visual reward for consistency.
Empty plot = no submissions
A flat dark tile means you didn't submit that day. Gaps in your habit are visible at a glance.
Columns = years
Each year is laid out as its own column, with months stacked front-to-back (January nearest, December furthest). Pink walls separate years.
